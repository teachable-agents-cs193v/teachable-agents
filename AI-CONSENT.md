# AI Consent

## Which third-party AI services receive user data

Groq runs our models. No identifying information about the user is sent to Groq.

## What we found in their data policies

Groq's policy is that they don't train on user data. They keep logs for abuse, but they don't train on them.

## How we present this to users

We present this to the user with the card before each session. We just don't provide the local agent option.

## Why we chose this way of informing users

We want to present users with a card so they're fully in control of their data.
