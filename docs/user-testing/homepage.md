# Homepage walkthrough notes

Arielle's notes from a user interview on the homepage.

* Read the headline, then looked at the big illustration in the hero. Glanced back at Protégé in the header, trying to figure out which name belonged to the app and whether the person in the drawing was Pip
* Noticed the green colors and notebook details while scrolling. Slowed down at the white text over the illustration and went back to read part of it again
* Stopped at Recent sessions while still signed out. Looked back toward the top of the page, unsure whether they were on the public homepage or a page meant for someone who already had an account
* Scrolled back up to find the main button. Paused before clicking Start teaching, unsure whether it would open the lesson list or ask them to make an account
* After signing in, landed on the lesson library and scanned the top of the page before looking at the cards. Spent a moment looking for a welcome or a clear first step before realizing they could start by choosing a lesson
* User went through demo environment about independence, saw the meeting recap (was impressed with it)
* Liked the concept, wished they had something similar growing up
