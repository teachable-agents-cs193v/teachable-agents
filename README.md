# Teachable Agents and the Protégé Effect

CS193V final project. *Working title.*

## Goal

An interactive, AI-powered learning tool that helps students understand a subject more deeply
by teaching it to someone else.

Students explain a concept to an AI character that acts as a virtual learner. The learner:

- asks questions,
- points out gaps in the explanation, and
- sometimes applies a concept incorrectly, so the student has to catch and correct its reasoning.

The tool scores the student's explanation against a simple rubric, gives feedback on areas that
need clarification, and lets students see how well they can apply what they've taught.

Making students responsible for teaching and correcting an AI learner should encourage active
learning, critical thinking and a stronger grasp of the concepts.

## Week 8 check-in target

A working prototype of the AI teaching agent. Students explain a concept in text, and the agent
responds with questions, shows what it understood, and sometimes misapplies what it learned so
the student can correct it. The prototype uses one sample topic and focuses on the core
interaction and feedback.
